KlarSheets – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

KlarSheets sells digital spreadsheet templates (Google Sheets and Excel) via Etsy (KlarSheetsShop) and Gumroad (klarsheets.gumroad.com). Contact: klarsheets@gmail.com

Pinterest app "KlarSheets Pin Publisher"

This app is a private tool used only by KlarSheets for its own Pinterest business account. It publishes and schedules KlarSheets' own pins to its own boards and reads analytics of its own pins. It does not access, collect, store or share data of any other Pinterest users. Access tokens are stored securely and used only for this purpose. Access can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest account settings.

Customers

Orders and payments are processed by Etsy and Gumroad under their own privacy policies. KlarSheets receives only the order data these platforms provide (such as product, price, country and email address) and uses it solely to deliver products, answer questions and keep simple sales records. Data is never sold or passed on to third parties.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data at any time by writing to klarsheets@gmail.com.
